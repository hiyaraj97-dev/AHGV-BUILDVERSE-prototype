1. The Fatal AI Gap & Innovation (Slide 1 & 4)

Standard AI chatbots in e-learning platforms search the entire course transcript indiscriminately. When a student pauses early in a lecture and asks a question, standard RAG retrieves future explanations, leaking future concepts, equations, and solutions.

The Mathematical Masking Rule:
Search Space = { Chunk_i | StartTime(Chunk_i) ≤ CurrentPauseTime }
Masked Space = { Chunk_j | StartTime(Chunk_j) > CurrentPauseTime } (Filtered BEFORE Vector Search)
